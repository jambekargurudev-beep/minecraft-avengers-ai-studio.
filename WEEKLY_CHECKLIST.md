# Weekly production checklist

- [ ] Monday: approve three story ideas and scripts
- [ ] Tuesday: publish Video 1
- [ ] Wednesday: create scenes and voice for Video 2
- [ ] Thursday: publish Video 2
- [ ] Friday: finish Video 3 and review all three edits
- [ ] Saturday: publish Video 3
- [ ] Sunday: record views, retention, CTR and comments; plan next week

## Before every upload
- [ ] Original/authorized visuals, voices and music
- [ ] Character consistency checked
- [ ] Captions checked
- [ ] Thumbnail readable on a phone
- [ ] Title and description reviewed
- [ ] Audience setting and made-for-kids status chosen accurately
- [ ] Final video watched from start to finish
